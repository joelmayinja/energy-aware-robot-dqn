How to read this experiment

Reward combines grid-energy penalty, peak-spike penalty, buffer charging/deployment, target distance and the task-completion bonus. ε is the chance of a random exploratory action. Loss is the DQN's Q-value prediction error; it is not an energy metric.

The baseline follows each target with inverse kinematics and maximum joint speed in the same seeded simulation. The 25 DQN actions are discrete velocity commands (one controlled joint at a time), not torque commands; the environment uses PyBullet velocity control. The 2D arm is a schematic of the real simulated joint angles, not a PyBullet camera render.

The DAEWOO steel dataset, student distillation, grid-fault scenarios and Raspberry Pi/ONNX execution are later project stages. This dashboard does not claim those are implemented or that a Raspberry Pi is connected.
