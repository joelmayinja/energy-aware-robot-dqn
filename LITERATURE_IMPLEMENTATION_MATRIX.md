# Literature-to-implementation matrix (working version)

This matrix records primary-source alignment for the V1.1 implementation. It is not a replacement systematic review; the submitted review manuscript and screening trail were not available to verify in this task.

| Topic / source | Method or finding relevant to this project | Current implementation | Evidence and gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep Q-learning: Mnih et al., “Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning,” Nature 518 (2015), DOI 10.1038/nature14236 ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25719670/)) | DQN combines neural value approximation and replay/target-network stabilization in Atari tasks. | V1.0 Q-network, replay buffer, target network; V1.1 experimental Double-DQN/Huber update is implemented separately. | This citation supports the algorithm family, not robot energy savings. V1.1 learning still needs executed training and independent evaluation.
| Policy distillation: Rusu et al., “Policy Distillation” (2015), [arXiv:1511.06295](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06295) | Transfers a teacher's action-value/policy distribution into a smaller model. | V1.1 collector stores observations, Q-values, greedy actions and trajectory IDs; student uses temperature KL, action CE and optional Q regression. | Closed-loop PyBullet retention must be measured. The earlier student result (6/50) remains historical, not replaced by loss alone.
| Sequential imitation and covariate shift: Ross, Gordon & Bagnell, “A Reduction of Imitation Learning and Structured Prediction to No-Regret Online Learning,” [arXiv:1011.0686](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686) | Learner-induced states can differ from expert demonstration states; dataset aggregation addresses the shift. | Initial V1.1 distillation uses teacher trajectories and trajectory-level splits. | Student-guided corrective collection is a future stage; any corrective data must retain teacher labels and provenance.

V1.1 buffer efficiencies are assumptions and must be sensitivity-tested; real robot data access is pending.

Even if acquired, UR electrical measurements do not validate KUKA iiwa without transfer analysis.

## Research alignment status

The current system is six controlled KUKA iiwa joints in PyBullet, with a 23-value state and 89 discrete velocity actions. It performs two-phase target reaching; it does not grasp or place an object. The steel dataset is exploratory demand context, not robot training data. No novelty claim is made. Stronger adaptive IK baselines, energy model validation, fresh held-out statistical evaluation, and measured hardware timing remain necessary before claims of practical efficiency or edge readiness.
